Cookie Policy

How SHDR uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential (always active) — required for login and core features.

  • shdr_session — maintains your login session.
  • shdr_otp_verified_* — tracks OTP verification.
  • shdr_language — remembers language (EN/RW/FR).
  • shdr_owner_remember — stores email for "Remember me".

Analytics (optional) — help us improve the Service.

  • _ga, _gid — Google Analytics.
  • _shdr_analytics — internal usage tracking.

Preference (optional) — remember your settings.

  • shdr_theme — dark/light mode.
  • shdr_dashboard_layout — dashboard customization.

3. How to Manage Cookies

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies.
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in.

4. Third-Party Cookies

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and data storage.
  • Google Analytics: anonymous usage statistics.
  • ImgBB: image hosting (no tracking).

Cookie Policy

How SHDR uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential (always active) — required for login and core features.

  • shdr_session — maintains your login session.
  • shdr_otp_verified_* — tracks OTP verification.
  • shdr_language — remembers language (EN/RW/FR).
  • shdr_owner_remember — stores email for "Remember me".

Analytics (optional) — help us improve the Service.

  • _ga, _gid — Google Analytics.
  • _shdr_analytics — internal usage tracking.

Preference (optional) — remember your settings.

  • shdr_theme — dark/light mode.
  • shdr_dashboard_layout — dashboard customization.

3. How to Manage Cookies

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies.
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in.

4. Third-Party Cookies
