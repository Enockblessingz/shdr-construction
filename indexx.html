Account Types
Two roles, one system

SHDR is built for two distinct users. Understand how each works before you begin.

Owner / Admin

Full project control

  • Sign Up for your own account directly
  • Create and manage multiple sites
  • Assign managers to specific projects
  • Receive complete reports for approval
  • Grant and revoke access anytime
Site Manager

On-site recording

  • Access granted by owner only
  • Cannot self-register — invitation required
  • Record materials, workers, progress
  • Submit daily updates and reports
  • Sign in only — no account creation
Managers sign in only. Your credentials are provided by the project owner.
